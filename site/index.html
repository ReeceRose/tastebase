Charred leek and white bean soup

Smoky, creamy and mostly pantry staples. Blending half the pot makes it thick without any cream.

Prep
15 min
Cook
30 min
Serves
4
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Serves 4

Method

  1. Heat a heavy pot over high heat. Char the leeks cut side down until black in spots, then slice them.

    8 min

  2. Lower the heat to medium. Add the oil, leeks, garlic and rosemary, and cook until the garlic is soft.

    5 min

  3. Add the beans and stock. Simmer until the beans turn creamy.

    12 min

  4. Blend half the soup and stir it back into the pot.

    5 min

  5. Stir in the lemon juice, salt and pepper. Taste and adjust.

  6. Ladle into bowls and serve hot.

Bring recipes in from anywhere.

Each one opens in the editor, so you can check it before it's saved.

  • A link. Reads the recipe from most sites and brings its photo along.
  • Text. Paste a recipe and it's split into ingredients and steps.
  • A photo. Snap a cookbook page and it's read for you.
  • The browser extension. Works on sites that block imports and on subscriber sites you're signed in to.
  • Your phone. Share a link straight in from Android or an iPhone Shortcut.

It runs on your machine, and that's the whole deal.

Runs on what you already have
A Synology, QNAP, Unraid or TrueNAS box, a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, or any Linux server with Docker.
Your data is yours
One SQLite database and a folder of photos, on your disk, easy to back up.
Free, with no subscription
Open source under the MIT licence. It doesn't need a cloud service, and everyone's account lives on your own server.

Install it.

You need a machine with Docker. Set a secret and the address you'll use in .env, start it, and create the first account.

Weeknight chana masala

The one we make when nobody planned dinner. Pantry chickpeas, a proper onion base and lemon at the end.

10 minPrep
20 minCook
EasyDifficulty
4.86 ratings

Ingredients

4

    Method

    5 steps · 30 min
    1. Warm the oil and fry the cumin seeds until they crackle.30 sec
    2. Add the onion with a pinch of salt. Cook until deep golden.8–10 min
    3. Stir in garlic, ginger and the ground spices for a minute so nothing catches.1 min
    4. Add tomatoes and chickpeas. Simmer, crushing a few to thicken.15 min
    5. Finish with lemon juice and a big handful of coriander.
